NOTE: This is Part 4 of my collection of key notes and learnings from the podcast episode 'The Courage To Be Transformed' by Dr. Robert Moore on Spotify. The link to the original podcast episode was located here . However, it seems that the audio has been deleted so I have made this writing to contain just the notes and an Index column.
Index
Notes
1
None of us is without a generator in there.
2
Humans create spirituality to deal with the problems of the world that can hinder the courage to be. There is no such thing as a non-practical spirituality, to the extent that it is an authentic spirituality and not a facade.
3
Authentic spiritual practice is an attempt to connect to those energies that serve life and to do so in such a way that they actually do serve life and don't turn demonic.
4
Religious forms can both connect you to the energy and they can get in the way of the energy.
5
Religious forms are ambivalent. You can tell whether a symbol is creative in a person's life by whether it is creative in a person's life.
6
Tillich says either the religious form promotes good like courage, peace, love, kindness, etc and whether it promotes bad like fear, anxiety, hate, etc. But the context matters. These are all different for different people, cultures, organisations, etc.
7
Tillich believes that every single Christian symbolism can be used in demonic ways. He was quite hard on Christianity but Tillich did believe in every tribe, all of their symbols can be used in demonic ways.
8
Alfred Adler once said that it did not matter what a person believed, but how they used it in their life. You do not examine the religious symbol itself but rather whether that symbol promotes life or promotes death.
9
An effective religion or spirituality must have power. Now whether that power is good or evil depends on a lot of things. In this day and age, young people subconsciously look at where there is power. Drug dealers, politics, etc are manifestations of the evil energy of society. They represent where there is power. Technology also plays a huge role. It can sometimes be easier to turn to technology uncritically and to avoid the kind of in-depth understanding of the spiritual life.
10
The question then becomes about discernment. How can you tell whether this thing is demonic or not.
11
How aware am I, practically speaking, of the power of Being all around me and in me? How aware am I of the power and presence of Non-Being in my world and in my personality and life?
12
Tillich thinks that at the level of essence, that is the intentionality of Being, the richness and power and fullness of Being in its primal state, we can imagine that we should be more than we are. When we are experiencing the feeling that we should be more than we are, we are intuiting the idea of essence that is related to the fullness of the primordial power of being. There is something real that is leading us to intuit we should be more than this.
13
Tillich would say that we have that sense that something is not right.
14
What kinds of anxieties can be fixed and what kinds must be understood. Then what does one do at that point?
15
The first primordial polarity of human experience is the experience of self and world together. From the moment we open our eyes when we are born, there is a world. We have got to differentiate ourselves from the world. The challenge is differentiating from a merger with world, people, things but also having to figure out how to maintain contact with the world. There are 3 other polarities that we need to think about too.
16
There is the polarity of freedom and the polarity of destiny. Destiny in this case has to do with the givenness of our lives that we have no control over. For example born into a certain family, into a certain social class, into a certain race. We can't control these things. Things that happened to our grand parents that affect us and we don't even know about them. The Freedom pole which is that no matter what my Destiny, there is always freedom of my response to this.
17
It is possible for trauma to destroy our humanity and can affect our Freedom pole. One of the questions we need to ask ourselves is where in my life are the structures of destruction that are trying to destroy my humanity? Depression is losing your sense of freedom. Despair is when one gets the idea or conclusion that their Freedom is gone. Despair is a rejection of one's humanness.
18
Grandiosity tries to tell us there is no destiny pole. It tells us we have unlimited freedom. Despair will try to tell us it is all destiny, that there is no freedom.
